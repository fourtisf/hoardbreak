HOW TO HEIST 🗝

1 · THE JOB. A dragon sleeps on a hoard deep in this lair. Today's lair is the same for every player — biggest heist tops the board. The golden arrow points to the gold.
2 · MOVE. Steer with the joystick / WASD — your lead thief (gold ▼) drives, the crew follows — and everyone fights automatically when guards come near. Or click to send them. Rooms hide chests, shrines with relics, armories… and sometimes a prison holding a fallen friend. Stand close to interact.
3 · STAY QUIET. Noise fills WYRM WAKE. At 50% one eye opens and it breathes in its sleep. At 75% guards stir. At 100% — it hunts. Items help: [1] Smoke [2] Lullaby [3] Bear Trap.
4 · GET OUT. Green arrow = time to run. Reach the EXIT tiles, press EXTRACT / E. Survivors gain XP. The dead end up in dragon prisons — go get them back.
