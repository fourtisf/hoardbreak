# @TheDragonjob — copy templates

The account **is** the game, not the person who built it. It talks the way the
lair talks: present tense, second person, short sentences, no adjectives doing
work a number could do better.

Everything below is a skeleton plus a worked example. Fill the skeleton from
the day's actual run — the numbers are the product, and they are all real and
checkable in-game.

---

## The never-write list

1. **First-person dev talk.** No "I built", "I made this", "my little game".
   The wyrm does not have a developer.
2. **"Check out" / "link in bio" / "drop a follow".** Ask for the thing itself,
   or ask for nothing.
3. **Hype adjectives.** No *insane*, *crazy*, *addictive*, *revolutionary*. A
   number is louder: `3,000 hp. You deal 59 a second. Do the arithmetic.`
4. **Emoji as decoration.** One, at most, and only where the game itself uses
   one (☠ ⚔ 🗝 💰). Never a row of them.
5. **Explaining the joke.** State the rule and stop. The cruelty lands on its
   own.
6. **Fake scarcity.** "Only 100 spots!!" — the invite gate is real, so let it
   speak for itself.

## 7 · The update (v0.4)

Four cards ship with this update, in `apps/web/public/brand/v04/`. Each one is
one beat, and each has its own post — an update is worth more than a single
announcement, and a card with no words under it is a poster nobody read.

Post them a day or two apart, in this order. The first is the announcement; the
rest are the reasons to come back.

## Reply-to-your-own-post

A reply carries the detail the parent post earned. Do not restate the parent —
add the next fact.

> **Parent:** The loot is the timer.
> **Reply:** And the timer is a face. It rolls over in its sleep, sweeps its
> tail across the pile, and the ring of gold that pays double moves with it.

---

## Length

One breath per line. If a line needs a comma to survive, it wants to be two
lines. Posts run 3–6 lines; anything longer is a thread, and a thread needs a
reason beyond having more to say.
