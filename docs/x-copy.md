# @TheDragonjob — copy templates

The account **is** the game, not the person who built it. It talks the way the
lair talks: present tense, second person, short sentences, no adjectives doing
work a number could do better.

Everything below is a skeleton plus a worked example. Fill the skeleton from
the day's actual run — the numbers are the product, and they are all real and
checkable in-game.

---

## The never-write list

1. **First-person dev talk.** No "I built", "I made this", "my little game".
   The wyrm does not have a developer.
2. **"Check out" / "link in bio" / "drop a follow".** Ask for the thing itself,
   or ask for nothing.
3. **Hype adjectives.** No *insane*, *crazy*, *addictive*, *revolutionary*. A
   number is louder: `3,000 hp. You deal 59 a second. Do the arithmetic.`
4. **Emoji as decoration.** One, at most, and only where the game itself uses
   one (☠ ⚔ 🗝 💰). Never a row of them.
5. **Explaining the joke.** State the rule and stop. The cruelty lands on its
   own.
6. **Fake scarcity.** "Only 100 spots!!" — the invite gate is real, so let it
   speak for itself.

## 8 · The token launch

`$DJOB` is earned by playing: `depth × 3`, double that past 60% of the hoard,
`+12` for a dead wyrm, `+1` per three guards. Top ten on the daily board split
`100 · 70 · 50 · 40 · 30 · 25 · 20 · 15 · 10 · 10` at rollover. Runs are capped
at **120 a day per wallet**, and a flagged run earns nothing.

Those numbers are the whole pitch. They are real, they are in the game, and a
reader can check every one of them tonight — which is more than most launches
can say. Lead with them.

### The extra never-write list, for these posts only

Everything in the list at the top still applies. These are additional.

1. **No price, ever.** No targets, no floors, no "still early", no charts, no
   market cap. The account does not know where the price is going and saying so
   anyway is how people lose money on your word.
2. **No manufactured urgency.** No countdowns to "the last chance", no "you will
   regret", no "before it runs". The daily reset is real urgency; nothing else
   has to be invented.
3. **No "utility" hand-waving.** Say the formula. If a thing is not built yet,
   say *not built yet*.
4. **No engagement farming.** No "like + RT for whitelist", no reply-to-enter.
5. **Never post the address as an image only.** People retype from screenshots
   and land on a fake. Text, every time, so it can be copied exactly.

## Reply-to-your-own-post

A reply carries the detail the parent post earned. Do not restate the parent —
add the next fact.

> **Parent:** The loot is the timer.
> **Reply:** And the timer is a face. It rolls over in its sleep, sweeps its

## Length

One breath per line. If a line needs a comma to survive, it wants to be two
lines. Posts run 3–6 lines; anything longer is a thread, and a thread needs a
reason beyond having more to say.
